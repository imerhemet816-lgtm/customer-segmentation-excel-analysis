# Customer Segmentation & Sales Performance Analysis

### Excel Data Analytics Portfolio Project

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer purchasing behavior, identifying valuable customer segments, and understanding sales performance using Microsoft Excel.

The objective is to transform raw customer and transaction data into meaningful business insights that can support marketing decisions, customer targeting, and sales strategies.

## Dataset

The analysis is based on two datasets containing:

- **10,000** customer records
- **20,000** sales transactions
- **8,682** customers with at least one purchase

The datasets include customer demographics, purchase dates, product information, and sales amounts.

## Business Objectives

- Identify customer segments based on demographic characteristics.
- Analyze purchasing frequency and customer spending.
- Evaluate sales performance across products and customer groups.
- Identify customer purchasing trends over time.
- Explore relationships between products purchased by the same customers.
- Provide actionable recommendations for marketing and sales improvement.

## Excel Workbook Structure

The Excel workbook includes the following analytical sheets:

1. **Overview** — Main KPIs and dashboard visualizations
2. **Customer Segments** — Demographic segmentation analysis
3. **Customer Metrics** — Purchasing frequency and spending patterns
4. **Sales Timeline** — Sales trends over time
5. **Weekday Analysis** — Sales distribution across weekdays
6. **Products** — Product performance analysis
7. **Cities** — Geographic sales analysis
8. **Product Affinity** — Products purchased by the same customers
9. **Product Triples** — Three-product purchasing patterns
10. **Data Audit** — Data quality checks and analytical limitations

## Key Business Insights

- Customers aged 30–44 in the female category represent the highest-revenue age/gender segment, generating approximately 2.58 million in sales.
- Qəhvə is the highest-revenue product, contributing approximately 2.55 million.
- Customer segmentation can help identify opportunities for more targeted marketing strategies.
- Product affinity analysis can support further investigation of potential cross-selling opportunities.

## Business Recommendations

**1. Targeted Marketing**

Develop marketing campaigns tailored to customer segments with strong purchasing activity.

**2. Product Strategy**

Review high-revenue products and investigate opportunities for personalized product recommendations.

**3. Customer Retention**

Use customer purchasing frequency and spending behavior to identify groups for potential loyalty campaigns.

**4. Improved Data Collection**

Introduce transaction timestamps and order identifiers to support more detailed hourly sales and market basket analysis.

## Data Limitations

The source dataset does not contain transaction times or basket/order identifiers.

Consequently, hourly purchasing patterns cannot be calculated, and product affinity analysis represents products purchased by the same customer over time rather than products purchased within the same transaction.
